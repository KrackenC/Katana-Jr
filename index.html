Katana Jr

Tools

New flight clears every check and the flight card.

Using it at the field

Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

  1. iPhone (Safari): Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android (Chrome): menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

Checks, your place in the Step view and the flight card are saved to your Claude account and to this phone, so closing and reopening the app picks up where you left off. The line under the progress bar shows which kind of saving is active. The page needs a connection to load, so open it once before you leave for the field, and turn on Screen on so it doesn't lock mid-check.

Kit-specific numbers (tube size, flying weight, charge sizes, pin count) come from your kit instructions. Beep codes, battery limits and ground-test steps follow the Altus Metrum EasyMini manual. If the kit instructions and this list disagree, the instructions win.
